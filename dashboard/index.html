How the radar scores

implied value = TVL × 0.082 × growth × maturity

The 0.082 is the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the 52 token-trading protocols in this snapshot — measured from the data, not guessed. Growth adjusts for the last 7 days of TVL change (clamped, so one wild week cannot dominate). Maturity rewards the protocols that have run longest with no token, up to two years. Exchanges, bridges and wrapped or staked products are set aside: they record no token because they are not protocols that issue one.

Implied values are this radar's own estimates from one snapshot. They do not predict whether or when any token is issued.
